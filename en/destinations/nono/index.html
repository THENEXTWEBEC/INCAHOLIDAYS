Nono Cloud Forest
The experience

Nono opens onto a greener, quieter Ecuador: folded cloud-forest valleys, hummingbirds, local food and the rhythms of a small Andean village.

Two- and three-day inclusive tours depart from Quito and can bring together local encounters, cycling, horseback riding, hiking, birdwatching, a private bird sanctuary, food experiences and overnight stays at Villa Doris.
