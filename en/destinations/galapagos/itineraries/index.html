GALÁPAGOS · ITINERARIES

Discover the islands in more detail.

Explore cruises, land-based expeditions and tailor-made experiences across the islands.

These are reference programs and sample routes. Itineraries may vary depending on departure dates, availability and weather conditions.

These world-famous islands are home to Ecuador’s first and largest national park, established in 1959. Located approximately 970 km off the coast of mainland Ecuador, the Galápagos archipelago comprises 13 major islands and more than 200 smaller islands, islets, and rocks.

Shaped by volcanic activity, ocean currents, climate, and the arrival of species from the mainland, the islands have developed an exceptional diversity of flora and fauna found nowhere else on Earth. Giant tortoises, blue-footed boobies, flightless cormorants, waved albatrosses, and marine iguanas thrive in this remarkable natural environment, often described as a living laboratory of evolution.

Yacht Catamaran Anahi

Experience (5 days / 4 nights)

Day 1: Baltra & Santa Cruz Islands: Bachas Beach

Upon arrival at Baltra Island, our guide will welcome you and escort you to Yacht Anahí. After boarding and settling into your cabin, enjoy lunch on board before visiting Bachas Beach, known for its white coral sand and sea turtle nesting sites. Explore a nearby lagoon, home to flamingos and shorebirds, and spot sea lions, marine iguanas, and seabirds along the coast. Return to the yacht for the welcome reception and dinner.

Day 2: Genovesa Island: Darwin Bay & Prince Philip's Steps

Discover Darwin Bay, renowned for its rich seabird diversity, including red-footed and Nazca boobies, swallow-tailed gulls, Galápagos petrels, seabirds, Darwin finches and mockingbirds. After lunch we will continue to Prince Philip's Steps, one of the island's most impressive bird nesting sites, with opportunities for hiking, snorkeling, kayaking, and take a ride in a panga.

Day 3: Plaza Islands & Santa Fe Island

Explore the Plaza Islands, known for their striking vegetation, sea lions, swallow-tailed gulls, red-beaked sparrows, frigate birds, pelicans and endemic land iguanas resting beneath cacti. Then we will return to the yacht for lunch and a deserved rest. Continue to Santa Fe Island, where scenic trails lead to beautiful viewpoints and opportunities to spot its endemic land iguanas. Enjoy additional opportunities for snorkeling, kayaking, and panga rides.Then we will return to the yacht for dinner.

Day 4: San Cristóbal Island: Punta Pitt, Kicker Rock & Lobos Island

Visit Punta Pitt, an important seabird nesting site where three species of frigatebirds can be observed. After lunch, continue to Kicker Rock (León Dormido), an impressive volcanic formation surrounded by blue-footed boobies, Nazca boobies, and other seabirds. At Lobos Island, enjoy a scenic walk among sea lions and nesting seabirds, followed by snorkeling in calm waters alongside colorful fish and playful sea lions.

Day 5: San Cristóbal Island: El Junco Lagoon or Cerro Colorado

Explore the highlands of San Cristóbal with a visit to either El Junco Lagoon, a freshwater lake nestled within an extinct volcanic crater, or Cerro Colorado Tortoise Breeding Center, where you can observe giant tortoises at different stages of life. Afterwards, transfer to the airport for your return flight to Quito or Guayaquil.

Experience (8 days / 7 nights)

Day 1- Santa Cruz Island: Highlands

Upon arrival at Baltra Airport, travel across the Itabaca Channel to the highlands of Santa Cruz Island. Enjoy lunch at a private ranch before exploring the lush forests, where you may encounter giant tortoises in their natural habitat. Visit a fascinating lava tunnel formed by ancient volcanic activity before heading to Puerto Ayora to board Yacht Anahí. Enjoy a welcome reception and dinner on board.

Day 2- Isabela Island: Las Tintoreras, Sierra Negra Volcano & Wetlands

Discover Las Tintoreras, a series of small islets surrounded by turquoise waters, where sea lions, marine iguanas, sea turtles, and rays can be spotted.

Next, we will walk along a path and explore two different beaches. The first beach is a popular place for sea lions and marine iguanas that nest in the area, while the second beach is surrounded by mangroves. We will disembark to Puerto Villamil and visit the impressive Sierra Negra Volcano, one of the most active volcanoes in the Galápagos. Later, explore the wetlands of Los Humedales, home to a variety of birdlife, and enjoy free time in Puerto Villamil. At the end of the day we will board the yacht for dinner and a well-deserved rest.

Day 3 –Isabela Island: Punta Moreno & Elizabeth Bay

Explore Punta Moreno through a scenic 2.1 km trail across volcanic landscapes, offering spectacular views of Isabela's active volcanoes (Sierra Negra and Cerro Azul) and Fernandina Island. Enjoy snorkeling in its rich marine environment before lunch. Then we will visit Elizabeth Bay. Navigate through red mangroves while observing sea turtles, flightless cormorants, rays, sea lions, fish schools and other marine and terrestrial wildlife. After this visit, we will return to the yacht for dinner and rest.

Day 4 – Isabela Island: Urbina Bay & Tagus Cove

Visit Urbina Bay located at the east base of the Alcedo volcano, where a scenic coastal trail offers opportunities to spot giant land iguanas, Galápagos tortoises, marine iguanas, penguins, blue-footed boobies and flightless cormorants. We will return to the yacht for lunch, followed by a short sailing towards Tagus Cove, for snorkeling or kayaking, followed by a hike to a viewpoint overlooking Darwin Lake, a unique saline lake surrounded by volcanic rocks and petrified rain.

Day 5 - Fernandina Island: Punta Espinoza & Isabela Island: Punta Vicente Roca

Explore Punta Espinoza on Fernandina Island, famous for its large colonies of marine iguanas, flightless cormorants, and Galápagos penguins, falcons and racer snakes. Enjoy snorkeling among marine life, with the possibility of encountering sea turtles and maybe even seahorses. Continue to Punta Vicente Roca on Isabela Island, where you will find clear turquoise water, an exceptional setting for snorkeling. Followed by a panga ride to observe the remains of the volcano, or we may explore a cave that is partially found underwater.Later, celebrate crossing the Equator with a special cocktail on board.

Day 6 – Santiago Island & Rábida Island

Discover Puerto Egas on Santiago Island, a striking black-sand beach where a coastal walk reveals sea lions, marine iguanas, and fascinating tidal pools. Enjoy snorkeling along its rugged coastline before visiting Rábida Island, famous for its distinctive red-sand beach and volcanic landscapes. When we arrive, we will enjoy a snorkeling activity, in order to learn about the marine life in this area, which is full of interesting aquatic species. Explore a scenic trail leading to a lagoon inhabited by flamingos, finches, pigeons, yellow warblers and mocking birds.

Day 7 – Sombrero Chino Islet & Bartolomé Island

Explore Sombrero Chino, a small volcanic islet known for its lava formations, sea lion colonies, and marine iguanas. Follow a scenic trail and enjoy opportunities for swimming, snorkeling, and kayaking in its beautiful bay where we will be amongst sharks, stingrays, unique species of fish, and occasionally penguins. Continue to Bartolomé Island, home to the iconic Pinnacle Rock. Discover its spectacular volcanic landscapes on a hike to panoramic viewpoints, followed by time to relax and snorkel at one of its beautiful beaches, where penguins, sea turtles, and colorful fish may be spotted.

Day 8 – North Seymour Island

Conclude your Galápagos adventure with a visit to North Seymour Island, renowned for its seabird colonies and unique wildlife. After exploring the island, transfer to Baltra Airport for your return flight, taking home unforgettable memories of the Galápagos Islands.
